<CaseSection label="Approach">

Riders don't linger on the category screen. They pick a ride and go. So the
shine had to be quick and fleeting: enough movement to draw the eye to the
trip, never enough to compete with the choice. I studied Uber's version first
and went more subtle. Ours also stays pinned to the map when you pan fast,
without the jelly wobble Uber's has.

</CaseSection>
