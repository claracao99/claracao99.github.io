A highlight moves along the route from pickup to destination. This provides a
directional cue, helping riders interpret the route and verify their ride
details before booking. The motion is deliberately restrained. It adds a sense
of activity while keeping attention on the primary tasks: selecting a ride
category and placing an order. The animation is optimised to preserve battery -
it disables in low battery mode, and stops after an extended time on the
screen.

The goal was to use motion as functional feedback and add delight to ride
hailing experience with minimal overhead.
