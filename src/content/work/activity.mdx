<CaseSection label="Research">

I ran usability testing on the first concepts, and it changed what I thought
the page was for. Riders weren't there to rebook. They came to check what
they'd spent, and business users came to find rides for their expense
reports. When people did want a past ride again, they typed it into "Where
to?". Analytics agreed: under 1% of rides came from rebook. So I designed the
tab for finding things, not repeating them.

</CaseSection>

<CaseSection
  label="Shipped"
  items={[
    'One feed for ongoing, upcoming and past rides, ordered by what matters now',
    'Easy-to-read prices, work tags and month headings',
    'Filters for service, profile and date, for everyone',
    'Rebook off the list, onto a featured card for the latest ride, next to rating',
    'Backend-driven, so the tab can change without an app release',
  ]}
/>

<CaseSection label="Results">

A two-week global A/B test came back clean: orders, GMV and cancellations held
flat. Riders opened past trips 23% more often (23.2% of visits, up from
18.8%), and the featured card drew more taps than every other row combined.
Activity rolled out to all riders in August 2026.

</CaseSection>
