The hard part was not the booking. It was the moment the car arrives and two
people need to know what is happening. The booker sees the trip in their own
app. The passenger gets a link, no install, with the driver, the plate and a
live map. The driver sees the passenger name, not the booker, so the kerb-side
conversation is right.

I prototyped the three screens as one tappable flow on Clay rather than as a
deck, and tested it with the rider support team first. Two rounds of that
changed the order of the confirmation screen and killed a feature we had
assumed was essential.
