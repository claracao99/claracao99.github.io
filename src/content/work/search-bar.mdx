<CaseSection label="Approach">

Pickup and destination sat in two separate fields, so nothing showed they
made one trip, or which way it went. Google Maps and Uber both draw a route as
one connected thing, a pattern riders already know. I reused the route
styling from the order flow rather than inventing new icons, so a route now
reads the same from search to receipt. And I left the background colour alone
on purpose, so the route stays the most prominent thing on screen.

</CaseSection>
